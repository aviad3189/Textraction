Textraction

SC–MC Text-Guided Extraction and Speaker Enhancement

Aviad Eisenberg  ·  Sharon Gannot  ·  Shlomo E. Chazan

Faculty of Engineering, Bar-Ilan University  ·  OriginAI, Israel

A single unified model that extracts a desired speaker — or any subset of speakers and noise sources — from a mixture, using only a natural-language prompt. The same network handles both single-channel and multi-channel inputs, conditions on either semantic attributes or relative spatial descriptions, and reduces to a state-of-the-art speech enhancer when only one speaker is present. Prompts are free-form and may specify entities to keep, to remove, or both.

Unified SC & MC

One architecture for single- and multi-microphone inputs, exploiting spatial cues when present.

Free-form prompts

Keep / remove any subset of up to two speakers and two noises — robust to paraphrasing.

Lightweight text encoder

A fine-tuned E5 retrieval model, contrastively tuned for keep-vs-remove polarity — no heavy LLM.

Relative spatial cues

Describes speakers by relative position (near/far, left/right) in plain language.

Overview & demo walkthrough
Model architecture Model architecture — RI-STFT U-Net with a text-conditioned (FiLM) transformer bottleneck.mdash; dedicated SC / MC encoders, a text-conditioned transformer bottleneck, and a U-Net decoder.

Two speakers and two noise sources are captured by a two-microphone array in a reverberant room. A free-form prompt names which entities to keep or remove, and the model reconstructs exactly that subset — exploiting spatial diversity when it is available.

Spatially-Guided Extraction

Here the prompt describes a speaker by relative position rather than voice attributes — e.g. “the voice closer to the array” or “the speaker on the left.” The model resolves the relative spatial relation between the two talkers.

Multi-Channel Enhancement

A single target speaker buried in heavy background noise, recorded on two microphones. The prompt simply asks to keep the speaker / remove the noise; enhancement is handled as a special case of the same extraction model. These examples were chosen for their very low input SNR to show the denoising under hard conditions.
